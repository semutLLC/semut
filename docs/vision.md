
## Vision

Semut LLC develops infrastructure for composing bounded
cybernetic systems across software, AI, hardware, humans,
and organizations.

Our core abstraction, CapabilityCell, represents system
inputs, states, transformations, boundaries, goals, and
validation through reusable contracts.

We investigate a common architecture for connecting
systems across domains without requiring them to share
the same internal implementation.

Our near-term focus is commercially useful applications.
Our long-term ambition is accessible, cross-domain
cybernetic infrastructure.

See [Vision and Validation](vision-and-validation.md)
for the scientific motivation, architecture, research
questions, and commercial validation strategy.
